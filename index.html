01About us

Design with purpose. Homes with heart.

Every house is special, and no two plots deserve the same plan. Pocket Home is a design and build practice built on that belief — every project starts as a brief, not a template.

The name comes from that idea: homes designed to fit your pocket — literally the plot, and financially the budget. Every square foot earns its place.

Studio based in Chennai.

02How we think

Every generation lives differently. Every house should too.

A hybrid-work couple needs a real corner for calls, not a spare room. A family with pets needs a home designed with them in mind. Every house has its own purpose — we solve it, from first sketch to final handover.

Hybrid work-life

A quiet, dedicated corner for calls — built into the plan, not squeezed in after move-in.

Pet-friendly design

Flooring, gates and outdoor access planned around the whole household — pets included.

Child-safe living

Safety considered at design stage — sightlines, railings and materials, not afterthoughts.

03Why now — Chennai

Chennai's plots are shrinking. The playbook hasn't.

Across OMR, GST Road, Tambaram and ECR, new layouts are trending smaller — yet most builders run the same plans regardless of plot size. Same proportions, same layouts, same wastage.
